tuulik

Notes at the speed of thought.

tuulik is the fastest way to get a thought out of your head and into the right place. If you keep losing ideas somewhere between the thought and the notetaking app, tuulik is for you.

Hold the pinwheel, flick toward a category, say or type the note. That is the whole thing.

What tuulik does not have: accounts, sharing, folders, tags, formatting, attachments, reminders, ads, analytics or subscriptions. It is free, and it is meant to stay small.
